Evaluated across real NASA 18650 cycling files under standard hardware noise (5 mV ADC noise + 30 mA current sensor DC offset). MLP 3-16-1 is recommended as the best overall algorithm for embedded edge BMS deployment. It delivers 1.25% average MAE, executes in 6.03 μs (zero matrix multiplications/inversions), fits into 348 bytes of Flash/RAM, and maintains strictly bounded error under sensor DC bias drift without requiring physical ECM parameter calibration.

Data: your NASA Li-ion cycling files (30 discharge runs at about 0.5, 1, 2 and 3 A and 30 CC-CV charge runs at 1, 2 and 3 A; Time, Current, Temperature, Vbat1-4). Cell voltage = mean of the four Vbat channels, averaged to a 5 s grid. SOC reference = coulomb counting normalised to each cycle's measured capacity (discharge 100% to 0%, charge 0% to 100%). The model was trained offline on 53 of the 60 files; discharge 02 and cycles 10, 20, 30 (discharge then charge) were held out and are what is replayed here. The virtual ESP32 adds ADC noise and a current-sensor offset on top of the real signals. 'Simulated cell' is the earlier synthetic twin.
