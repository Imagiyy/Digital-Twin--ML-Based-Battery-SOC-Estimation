State of Health (SOH) Estimation: Capacity Fade & Resistance Growth

Battery aging degradation manifests primarily through two concurrent physical mechanisms: Capacity Fade (loss of active lithium ions) and Resistance Fade (thickening of the Solid Electrolyte Interphase layer). The Digital Twin implements dual real-time tracking for both phenomena.

1. Capacity Fade Algorithm (SOHC)

SOHC = (Cactual / Cnominal) × 100%

Nominal fresh capacity = 2.65 Ah. During cycling, Ah-throughput accumulation ΔC = α · √(Ahthroughput) tracks active lithium inventory loss. At full cycle completions, trapezoidal integration updates Cactual.

2. Resistance Fade Algorithm (SOHR)

SOHR = [(Reol - R0) / (Reol - Rfresh)] × 100%

Fresh ohmic resistance Rfresh = 55 mΩ; End-of-Life Reol = 110 mΩ (100% resistance growth). Dynamically extracts ΔV / ΔI during current steps and applies exponential recursive filtering.

Dual Metric Fusion & Remaining Useful Life (RUL)

SOHoverall = 0.60 · SOHC + 0.40 · SOHR · RUL: Cycles remaining until reaching the standard 80.0% End-of-Life (EOL) retirement threshold.

Physical Principles & SOC Definition

State of Charge is defined via Coulomb Counting integration:

SOC(t) = SOC(0) - (1 / C_nominal) * ∫ i(t) dt

Why ML? Coulomb counting accumulates DC sensor offset (±30 mA) over time, and OCV lookup requires long relaxation rests. An MLP learns the non-linear terminal voltage-to-SOC mapping directly without requiring equivalent circuit modeling.

Known Limitations & Future Scope

  • Cycle vs Cell Generalisation: Held-out test files originate from the same physical cells under different cycling currents.
  • Label Derivation: SOC labels are normalized from measured Coulomb counting capacity, inheriting its fundamental bounds.
  • Future Scope: Adding cell temperature as a 4th input, cross-cell transfer learning, SOH aging degradation estimation, and physical ESP32 HIL deployment.
